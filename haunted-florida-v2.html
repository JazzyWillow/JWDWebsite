Where History Meets the Unexplained

Haunted Florida blends real-world exploration with immersive storytelling. These hand-crafted wooden travel maps are more than décor—they invite you to explore the legends, mysteries, and folklore woven into the state’s past.

Each map includes a bag of wooden ghost markers. As you visit a destination, place a ghost into its spot on the map to mark where you’ve been. Tap your phone to the map’s Story Tag to discover curated historical background, local legends, and official visitor information connected to each eerie destination.
